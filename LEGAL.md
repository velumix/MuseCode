## Application source license

This repository currently has **no project software license**. Public source,
a download link, and a terms draft do not establish an open source license.
No new permission to modify or redistribute Velum-owned source or brand assets
is granted by these draft documents. Rights granted by GitHub's own terms,
mandatory law, or a separate agreement are unaffected.

The publisher must choose and approve a project license before distributing
the project as open source. If the app will be proprietary, the publisher must
instead finalize its executable license and distribution terms. The plugin SDK
also needs an explicit licensing decision before publishing it as a package.
Third-party software retains its own licenses regardless of this choice.

## Independent product

Velum Code is not an official product of, or represented as endorsed by, the
operators of Muse, OpenAI Codex, Google Antigravity, Microsoft, Tailscale or GitHub.
Those services and their marks belong to their respective owners. Provider
accounts, usage limits, charges and policies are separate from Velum Code.

## Before release

Complete [legal/publisher.json](legal/publisher.json), resolve the decisions in
the [release guide](docs/legal-release.md), and replace every bracketed draft
field. Retain a copy of each final terms version. Publishing these files alone
does not establish that a user accepted an agreement.

No document can eliminate every claim or guarantee enforceability. The final
documents and distribution process need review for the publisher's business
and the countries where the app will be offered.
